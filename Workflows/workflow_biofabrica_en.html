Design notes
  • Dispatch and automatic inventory: Every dispatch — whether to Food Production, Nursery, Landscaping, or an External Client — is registered by Kennedy in the portal. The moment the dispatch is logged, the system automatically debits the Finished Product Inventory. No manual stock adjustment needed.
  • Batch ID: Each production batch generates a unique identifier for traceability — which raw materials were consumed, start and end dates, actual vs. estimated yield, and who executed it.
  • Production triggers: The production cycle can be activated by any of the four clients: Food Production (Tomás, weekly order on Thu/Fri), Nursery (Dani), Landscaping (Alejandro), or External Clients. It can also be triggered automatically by low-stock alerts from the Raw Materials Inventory.
  • Calculated inventory: Inventories are never entered manually — they are calculated in real time by adding entries and subtracting batch consumptions and product dispatches. The system shows alerts when any item falls below the configured minimum stock.
  • Continuous cycle: After closing a batch, Kennedy reviews inventory and pending orders and opens new batches as needed. The diagram represents one typical production cycle — in practice this loop runs continuously.
