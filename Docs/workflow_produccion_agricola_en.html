Design notes
  • Lot ID: Each sowing event generates a unique identifier (Lot ID) that travels with the crop through all subsequent records — input applications, availability forecasts, and harvests. This enables full traceability from field to kitchen delivery.
  • Planning lead time: The Weekly Plan Review happens the prior Thursday or Friday so that by Monday morning the whole team already knows what to plant. Nursery orders are placed 3–4 weeks ahead; Biofactory inputs orders are placed one week ahead.
  • Biofactory order: The dashed arrow from "Weekly Plan Review" to Biofactory activates a separate workflow within that department — documented in the Biofactory workflow map.
